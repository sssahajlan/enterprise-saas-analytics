# Business Requirements

## Company Overview

The company is a fictional B2B SaaS business that provides project management and workflow software to other companies.

## Company Name

Kraly

### Mission

Help organizations improve productivity by providing an intuitive platform for managing projects, tasks, collaboration, and reporting.

---

## Customers

The customers are businesses that purchase subscriptions for their employees.

Customer companies will be fictional organizations across multiple industries, including technology, healthcare, finance, retail, education, manufacturing, and logistics.

---

## Users

Users are employees of customer companies who use the platform to manage projects, tasks, files, reporting, and collaboration.

Typical roles include:
- Software Engineers
- Product Managers
- Data Analysts
- Human Resources
- Finance
- Marketing

---

## Subscription Plans

- Starter
- Professional
- Enterprise

## Core Product Features

- Project management
- Task management

## Business Questions

### Executive Leadership

- How much monthly recurring revenue does Kraly generate?
- How has revenue changed over time?
- Which subscription plan generates the most revenue?
- What is the monthly customer churn rate?

### Product Team

- Which product features are used the most?
- Which features have low adoption?
- How many users are active daily, weekly, and monthly?
- Which user actions are associated with higher retention?

### Customer Success Team

- Which customer accounts are at risk of churning?
- Which customers have not logged in recently?
- Which customers submit the most support tickets?
- How long does it take to resolve support tickets?

### Sales and Finance

- Which industries generate the most revenue?
- What is the average revenue per customer?
- Which customers have overdue invoices?
- How many customers upgrade or downgrade their plans each month?
